## Environment prompts (one full-size landscape each, never a collage)

**01 · Main menu / Living Playroom.** Title composition, low camera at tabletop height. The toy
village on the desk at golden dusk; MASCOT standing proudly on a wooden block plinth in the
foreground; bunting, domino walls, toy train; calm empty space upper-left for the logo. STYLE.

**02 · Playroom village hub.** Third-person camera behind MASCOT on a felt playmat square: striped
cardboard gift-box tournament pavilion with bottle-cap finial; open wooden pencil-case workshop with
thread-spool chimney puffing cotton smoke; matchbox shop with drawer counter and striped awning;
domino scoreboard holding a big painted card; three building-block arches with coral, honey and blue
flags (honey one glowing, a small brass bell inside); bottle-cap fountain with a marble on a thread
spool; toy train on an oval wooden track; dominoes and a fallen yellow pencil as the fence at the
table edge. STYLE.

**03 · Copycat Conductor: mechanical music theatre.** Camera gently elevated, facing the stage. A
cigar-box and hardback-book stage with ink-purple and coral felt curtains, bead footlights, a
cardboard proscenium with painted gold trim. MASCOT as conductor on a podium raising a toothpick
baton. In front, four big toy instruments in a row: coral toy drum, golden bell on a wooden stand,
toy-blue chunky xylophone, sage-green mechanical music box with crank and brass comb; the golden
bell glowing and vibrating. Domino audience seating, thread spools, bunting. STYLE.

**04 · Inferno Escape: cardboard labyrinth.** Top-down three-quarter view of a maze built from
corrugated cardboard strips and egg-carton walls on a baking tray; crumbly sugar-cube and cork
"diggable" blocks; one finished protective wall of stacked dominoes; at one edge a creeping orange
glow from a hot-water bottle and a toaster towering in the background, warm red-orange light raking
across. Several contenders mid-dig. STYLE.

**05 · Midnight Hunger: wind-up survival arena.** The same tabletop at night: blue moonlight through
the window, the desk lamp switched off, a phone-screen glow and a nightlight as the only warm
sources. A walled arena built from a lunchbox and biscuit-tin lids, scattered crumbs, raisins and
cereal as resources, shadowy sock-puppet silhouettes beyond the lamp light. Ink purple and toy blue
dominate, honey only where light falls. STYLE (night variant).

**06 · Find the Original: toy-collection shelf.** A bookshelf ledge seen at toy height packed with
dozens of near-identical wind-up robots in slightly different paint jobs and accessories, lined up like a
museum display on felt risers with little paper name tags (blank), one spotlight from a
clip-on reading lamp. STYLE.

**07 · Red Light, Green Light: toy runway.** A long straight track made from a wooden ruler and
masking-tape lanes across the desk, start line of matchsticks, finish line under the desk lamp; a
traffic-light made from three bottle caps on a spool tower (red lit); contenders frozen mid-step,
one wobbling. STYLE.

**08 · Blink Duel: miniature battle arena.** A circular arena on an upturned cake tin with chalk
markings, teleport pads made of shiny coins, low cover from thimbles and erasers, two contenders
mid-duel with streaks of honey and coral light, cushions and blankets looming around. STYLE.

**09 · Wind-Up Dash: obstacle course.** A winding race route across books, a stapler bridge,
a ramp of rulers, rolling marbles, swinging clothespins, snapping mousetrap (comic, harmless) and a
fan made from a desk fan's blurred blades in the distance; contenders sprinting with keys spinning.
STYLE.

**10 · Music Box Rhythm: performance stage.** The inside of an open giant music box as a stage:
brass comb as a backdrop, rotating pinned cylinder, four glowing lanes (coral, honey, blue, sage)
running toward the camera; mirror on the lid reflecting the room; audience of felt animals.
STYLE.

**11 · Toy Squad: miniature strategy board.** An old wooden board-game board (checker grid) used as a
battlefield, squads of tiny toys (wind-up robots, plastic animals, building-block golems) placed
on squares, dice and game tokens as terrain, a card-game hand fanned at the side. Slightly higher
camera for readability. STYLE.

**12 · Spellbox Showdown: magical toy arena.** A round arena on a jewellery box lid with a velvet
floor, edges falling away to the table; contenders casting spells shaped like glowing marbles and
paper stars; knockback rings; fairy-light strands overhead; the lamp tinted slightly ink purple.
STYLE.

**13 · Jack-in-the-Box: the biscuit-tin ring.** Wind-up toys sitting in a circle on an old blue
biscuit-tin lid lined with purple felt; a painted wind-up box with a brass crank in the middle, lid
bursting open as a cheerful jack on a spring leans out at one startled toy; speech-bubble cards
floating over the others; warm lamp light. STYLE.
