# Base44 x Nitro submission brief

Purpose: build an **original Base44-platform** version of the Toybox Trials concept, sized for a
prompt-driven build and the 3 Dec 2026 deadline. This document is not part of the game; it is the
input for a new app on Base44 (starting from `base44.com/nitro`), which is what the contest rules
require (§5.1, §6.1b, §6.2: built on Base44, live at a stable public URL, project history
available to the judges).

## Rules that shape the build

- Browser game, desktop **and** mobile, no download, install, plug-in or extension.
- Free in full, **no login, no paywall, no in-game purchase**. Public app.
- New build during 5 Nov – 3 Dec 2026. Original work only: no third-party characters, art, music,
  names or likenesses. AI assets must be licensed for this use.
- Must stay live until judging ends and for at least 90 days after winners are announced.
- One entry per person/team (up to 4). Judged on gameplay quality, creativity and technical
  execution, equally weighted.

## Scope that fits the time (recommended)

Build the **hub + three trials**, not eleven. A short, polished, replayable loop beats a wide,
half-finished one, and the judges weight polish heavily.

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Playroom hub | One tabletop scene, three lit doors/gates, a scoreboard. Player moves a small toy with WASD/arrows or a thumb joystick. |
| Copycat Conductor | Four instrument pads; growing sequence; one wrong note or timeout eliminates; 1 player + up to 7 bots. |
| Jack-in-the-Box | Category prompts; type an answer nobody has said; correct answer cranks and passes the box; hidden fuse pops one player; last toy wins. |
| Wind-Up Dash | Short side-on sprint with obstacles and a wind-up speed budget; best time + ghosts-free time trial against bots. |
| Results | Placement, per-trial best, "play again" and "next trial". |

Deliberately out of scope for the submission version: online multiplayer, accounts, ranked play,
shop/economy, tournaments beyond a simple 3-trial run.

## Build brief to paste into Base44

> Build a single-page browser party game called **Toybox Trials: The Living Playroom**, mobile and
> desktop, no login. Art direction: a warm, handcrafted miniature toy village on a wooden tabletop
> inside a huge playroom at night; chunky rounded shapes, felt and painted-wood materials, honey,
> coral, sage and toy-blue palette, soft window light, dusty air. Rounded display font for
> headings, clean sans for body. Everything is original — no existing game characters, logos,
> music or famous toy brands.
>
> Screens: (1) Playroom hub — a tabletop scene with three doors, a scoreboard and a settings gear.
> Move a small wind-up toy with WASD/arrows or an on-screen thumb joystick; tap/Enter to enter a
> door. (2) Copycat Conductor — four large pads (A/S/J/K and on-screen), a conductor plays a
> sequence that grows by one note each round; repeat it in order; a wrong note or running out of
> time eliminates you; last toy standing wins; 1 human + up to 7 bots. (3) Jack-in-the-Box — each
> round shows a category ("Name a fruit"); type an answer that fits and has not been used; a good
> answer cranks the box and passes it to the next player; a hidden fuse pops the holder out; last
> toy in wins. (4) Wind-Up Dash — short side-on sprint, jump and duck obstacles, a wind-up meter
> that drains while sprinting; finish fastest. (5) Results screen with placements, best scores and
> a play-again button.
>
> Requirements: all sound synthesised in the browser (no audio files); large tap targets (56px+),
> safe-area padding, portrait and landscape; save best scores and settings locally; no sign-in, no
> network calls, no analytics; start playing within one tap of load; under 5 MB total assets;
> visible keyboard and touch controls on every screen; pause with Esc or a gear button.

## Originality and assets

- No third-party IP: no licensed characters, logos, sports teams, famous toys, or recognisable
  music. All art generated for this project, or drawn/rendered by you.
- Keep an asset log (file, tool/account, licence, date) from the first asset — the rules let the
  organisers ask for provenance and disqualify a submission that can't show it.
- Check the generating tool's commercial-use terms **before** shipping an asset: our manifest still
  marks the Higgsfield concept art and the room GLBs as "confirm before public/competition use".
- Music for the rhythm trial must be composed/commissioned by you, or explicitly licensed and
  logged. Synthesised effects avoid the question entirely.

## Submission checklist

1. App is public and loads with no sign-in on a phone and a desktop browser.
2. Stable public URL recorded; test it in a private window with no cookies.
3. Title, one-line pitch, description, thumbnail, and the Base44 account used.
4. Enter on Launchpad (community vote) and on the Nitro contest page, if going for both pools.
5. Deadline: **3 Dec 2026, 23:59 UTC** (≈ 4 Dec, 10:59 AEDT).
6. After judging opens, don't materially change or unpublish the game.
