# Toy Squad catalogue

62 original toy units. Costs are fixed by rarity (1–5 coins). Stats below are for one star; two equal-star copies merge, up to three stars. Health and attack damage double per star. Armour and ability tuning are provisional.

Combat uses 8×8 grid range (including diagonals). The full ability descriptions appear below the roster.
Guard units have armour equal to their rarity tier (1–5); other units start with zero armour.

## Ability rules

- **strike:** Accurate single-target attacks.
- **guard:** Armour reduces each incoming hit (minimum 1).
- **cleave:** Also hits enemies next to its target for half damage.
- **splash:** A blast hits enemies within one square of its target for half damage.
- **poison:** Hits poison the target for 1 damage on the next 3 combat ticks; refreshes, does not stack.
- **stun:** Every third attack stuns its target for the next tick.
- **heal:** Every third attack also heals the most wounded ally.
- **drain:** Heals itself for one third of attack damage.
- **execute:** Deals double damage to targets below one third health.
- **chain:** Bounces half damage to one additional nearby enemy.
- **pierce:** Ignores armour, and hits one enemy directly behind the target.
- **fury:** Every third attack deals double damage.

## Passive choices

Choose one of three unique offers on rounds 3, 6 and 9. Chosen effects persist for the run and are not offered again. If the build timer expires without a selection, the first offer is selected automatically.

| Passive | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Felt Padding | All units gain 4 maximum health. |
| Tight Springs | All attacks gain 1 damage. |
| Tin Polish | All units gain 1 armour; each hit still deals at least 1. |
| Button Sights | Ranged units gain 1 attack range. |
| Repair Kit | Every fourth tick, living units recover 2 health. |
| Bubble Wrap | Each unit starts every battle with a 5-point shield. |
| Pocket Money | Gain 2 extra coins at the start of future rounds. |
| Bargain Bin | Shop rerolls cost 1 coin less (minimum 1). |
| Wooden Vanguard | Units deployed in the front two rows gain 6 health. |
| Back-row Barrage | Units deployed in the back two rows gain 1 damage. |
| Clockwork Siphon | Attacking units recover 1 health per successful attack. |
| Music-box Encore | Every fourth attack deals 2 extra damage. |
